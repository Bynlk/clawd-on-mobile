# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers who run local AI coding agents and use Clawd on Desktop with the native Android companion. They may leave their desk while an agent is working and need a dependable way to inspect sessions, answer permissions, and reconnect through a self-hosted VPS.

## Product Purpose

Clawd makes local AI-agent activity visible and controllable without replacing the user's terminal. Remote Connection performs a one-time VPS deployment, then reduces daily remote access to connecting this computer and opening the Android app. Success means the common path is obvious, secrets stay local, and failures explain the single next action.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, direct. The desktop pet may be playful, but settings and remote-access controls should feel precise and operational.

## Anti-references

- Hosting dashboards that expose networking internals during routine use.
- Persistent multi-step installers after installation has finished or failed.
- Dense cards with several equally prominent actions.
- Repeated warning and error banners describing the same condition.
- Jargon-heavy VPN control panels that require users to understand WireGuard topology.

## Design Principles

1. Optimize the daily path after completing the one-time setup.
2. Separate VPS deployment, this-computer connection, and Android pairing as distinct states.
3. Show one primary action for the current state and progressively disclose maintenance actions.
4. Give one actionable explanation for each failure; do not stack duplicate status messages.
5. Keep security boundaries explicit without making secrets or network internals routine UI.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast. All actions must be keyboard accessible, focus-visible, and understandable without color. Respect reduced motion, associate errors and hints with their controls, and keep localized text usable at increased text scale.
